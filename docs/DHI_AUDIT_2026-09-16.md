# Mathematical audit of the DHI model, 16 September 2026

Scope: the probabilistic architecture of tab 5.1 as implemented in `hcwc/core/dhi.py`,
`hcwc/core/well.py` and `hcwc/ui/dhi_tab.py`, against the intended chain

```
A.  P(G | strength)
B.  p(h | G, geometry)  ∝  p(h | G) · L(geometry | h, G)
C.  POS(h) = P(G | strength) · P(H ≥ h | G, geometry)
```

with one set of posterior weights from B supplying the histogram, the percentiles, the
exceedance curve and the conditional term in C. Verdict first, then the nine items. Tests are in
`tests/test_dhi_audit.py` (22 tests) and the pre-existing `tests/test_dhi.py`.

**Verdict: no mathematical inconsistency found. No redesign.** Three documentation defects were
corrected (a wrong symbol in one docstring, and two assumptions that were implemented but not
stated), and one UI sentence was added. Nothing numerical changed.

## 1 · p_valid

`p_valid` is `P(the picked event is the HCWC | G, contact attributes)`. On the tab it is the
slider or the geometric mean of the three graded attributes, clipped to [0.01, 0.99]
(`dhi_tab.py`, `p_valid = clip(contact_given_hc, 0.01, 0.99)`). Nothing derived from the
strength enters it; `likelihood()` takes no strength argument, so the signature is the proof.
Pinned: `TestPValidIsAContactAttributeJudgementOnly`, and the older
`TestStrengthCannotReachTheGeometryPosterior`, which reproduces the pre-14-Sep leak on purpose.

## 2 · Seen likelihood

```
L = c · D(h) · f_pick(z | h) + (1 − c) · s,     s = 1 / (deep − shallow)
```

Both branches are densities in depth: `f_pick` integrates to one over depth (normal, PERT or
uniform), `s` integrates to one over the declared support. `s` comes from
`LimitSet.contact_support_m()`, apex to the tightest always-active limit, not from the sampled
min/max; the sampled range differs from it and the likelihood is invariant to the trial count
and seed (`TestTheSpuriousDensityIsAPropertyOfTheModel`, since 14 Sep). The floor
`L / s ≥ 1 − c` holds everywhere, with equality far from the pick.

Defect corrected: the `likelihood()` docstring wrote the spurious branch as `(1 − p_valid) · c`
and the floor as `L / c`, reusing `c` for two things. Now `s`, with its definition beside it.

## 3 · Partial conformance

```
L = c · D(h) · Φ((z_off − z) / σ) + (1 − c) · 1
```

Both branches are probabilities of the event *edge recorded above z_off*, not densities, so the
mixture is coherent within itself and the spurious branch is correctly the constant 1 (the
event's probability in the world where the bright thing is not the column, taken as certain:
the highest floor the mixture can have, the conservative choice). It is a soft, censored
constraint on the depth of the anomaly's edge with one parameter, the edge error σ, and no
realisation is assigned zero. It is not a forward model of the amplitude response. The docstring
and 8.1.6 now say so in those words. Pinned: `TestPartialConformanceIsASoftCensoredBound`.

## 4 · Absence

Within G the weights are `1 − D(h)` and nothing else. On G the chance is updated by
`R_absent = (1 − d) / (1 − f·d)`, `d = E[D(h)]` over the geological columns, clipped to
[1/10, 1]; the strength is ignored when nothing was seen (`applied_ratio`). `f`, the barren
trap's chance of showing relative to a filled one, is elicited and uncalibrated; the widget help,
the 5.1.6 list and 8.1.6 label it so, and the default 0.5 is labelled maximum-ignorance.
Pinned: `TestAbsenceIsSplitBetweenTheTwoChannels`, `TestAbsenceLowersTheChance`.

## 5 · Strength

`P(G | strength) = R·P(G) / (R·P(G) + 1 − P(G))` with `R` capped at 10 : 1 either way (Simm's
single-channel ceiling). At the axis end `R = 10`, so from `P(G) = 0.408` the chance rises to
0.873, not to 1: one line of evidence cannot make the prospect certain, by design. The
posterior contact weights are identical at every strength, so the P90–P10 spread of the contact
is unchanged by it and is a real number of metres. Pinned: `TestStrengthMovesTheChanceAndNotTheDepth`.

## 6 · Contact validity

One `c` weights the mixture for every realisation; solving the likelihood for `c` per
realisation returns the typed number. The assumption that `c` does not depend on `h` was
implemented and not stated; it is now stated on `DhiObservation`, in 8.1.6 and in this note.
The column height enters the valid branch through `D(h)` only.

## 7 · Dependence

There is no dependence parameter in the chance. `CombinedUpdate.dependence` survives only for
the teaching comparison of the blended construction and reaches no number on the tab (grep:
no UI reference). The two channels multiply as separate factors; their relation is at
elicitation, since body and contact attributes both improve with impedance contrast, and that is
a heuristic judgement by the assessor, not a fitted joint distribution. Now stated in a code
comment at 5b and in the 5b sentence on screen; 8.1.5 already said it.

## 8 · Limiting cases

All pass (`tests/test_dhi_audit.py`):

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| neutral strength | `P(G)` unchanged; POS = `P(G) × F_post(h_min)` to 1e-15 |
| strongest strength (R = 10) | `P(G)` → 0.873 from 0.408; contact spread unchanged, > 10 m |
| weak DHI (σ = 200 m, c = 0.05, weak reading) | column term moves < 0.02, P50 < 5 m, `P(G)` < 0.02 |
| `p_valid → 1` | weights = `D(h) · f_pick` exactly |
| `p_valid → 0` | column term within 1e-3 of the prior, spread within 1 m |
| very broad pick (σ = 5 km) | posterior = prior within 0.02 / 5 m |
| very narrow pick (σ = 0.5 m) | posterior concentrates; every weight > 0; spread > 0 |
| neutral geometry likelihood | percentiles, exceedance and POS equal the prior to 1e-12 |
| curve at `h_min` | equals the headline to 1e-15 |

## 9 · Critical consistency identity

In the core: `prospect_pos = p_g_given_strength × Σw[h ≥ h_min] / Σw` to 1e-12, and
`prospect_pos_curve(h_min)` equals it to 1e-15. On the rendered tab: the overlay's
`posterior_pos` equals `p_g_given_amplitude × F_post(h_min)` computed from the overlay's own
weights on the run's realisations to 1e-12; the stored weights are the posterior's weights; the
chance curve interpolated at `h_min` equals the headline; and the bars of the posterior
histogram (Figure 5.1.4) are `np.histogram(contact_m, weights=w) / Σ` to 1e-12.
Pinned: `TestTheCriticalConsistencyIdentity`.

## Notes outside the nine items

- Percentiles are reported over the success cases (h ≥ h_min) with the same weights, while
  `F_post` is over all realisations conditional on G. Both read the same weights; the
  restriction is the stated convention for contact percentiles and is not a second posterior.
- Well control multiplies the geometry weights by its own likelihood before the posterior is
  stored, so the stored posterior, the overlay and the headline all carry it.
- `p_valid` is clipped to 0.99 on the tab although the core accepts 1.0; the clip is Cromwell's
  rule applied on entry.
