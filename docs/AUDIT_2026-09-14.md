# Numerical audit, 14 September 2026

A full read of the eleven core modules and the eight core test files, with numerical probes where
reading could not settle a question. Two findings were defects and are fixed in the same commit as
this document; the rest are limitations, robustness issues or polish, and are recorded here rather
than acted on.

Modules read: `engine`, `limits`, `dists`, `charge`, `seals`, `correlate`, `censoring`,
`calibration`, `decompose`, `dhi`, `well`. Tests read: `test_engine`, `test_dhi`, `test_charge`,
`test_seals`, `test_correlate`, `test_censoring`, `test_decompose`, `test_well`.

Classification: **P0** mathematical or conceptual error · **P1** scientifically important
limitation · **P2** numerical or UX robustness · **P3** documentation or style.

## Verified correct, not to be changed

- **Engine.** Minimum over active limits with a `+inf` sentinel; the apex as column 0 of the copula; depth-stated limits converted against the same realisation's apex and refused rather than clipped when negative; `>=` used consistently for `above_minimum`, `exceedance` and `pos`; success-only percentiles with P90 the shallow end; weighted exceedance by `searchsorted` identical to the broadcast form; same seed, same run.
- **Copula.** Spearman to Pearson by `2 sin(πρ/6)`, exact; Higham projection converges, and the SCOPE-HC defect is avoided; requested 0.9 realised as 0.898.
- **Distributions.** `RiskBetaSubj`, PERT and NormalAlt parameterisations and their quantile and density forms; bounded densities return exact zero outside support.
- **Charge.** km² × m = 10⁶ m³; `V_res = STOIIP × Bo`, `V_res = GIIP / (1/Bg)`; non-limiting charge returns `inf`, goes to `p_active`, and the column table is built from the limiting subset only, which is the right conditional.
- **Capillary seal.** `Pc = 2γ cos θ / r` with dyne/cm × 10⁻³; `h = Pc / (Δρ g)`; the reservoir's entry pressure subtracted with the same γ and θ per realisation.
- **Mechanical seal.** `H = (S_Hmin − P_p) / ((ρ_w − ρ_hc) × 0.0980665)`, bar over bar per metre; 10 197 m per bar per 0.1 g/cm³ pinned.
- **Censoring.** Right-censored log-linear maximum likelihood with a density term for underfilled points and a survival term for filled-to-spill points; the naive and filtered slopes shown to fail.
- **Calibration.** Exceedance-percentile convention consistent with the rest of the tool: a deeper built P50 lands at a lower benchmark percentile and reads as optimistic.
- **DHI**, after the 14 Sep corrections. `p_valid` conditional on G; `POS = P(G | strength) × F_post(h_min)`; one weight vector for the histogram, the percentiles and the chance; spurious density from the declared support; the bound as a censored pick; Cromwell floors on both channels.
- **Well.** Censored-normal steps with a `p_connected` floor; multiplied into the DHI weights as independent evidence.
- **Decomposition.** Group minima; the column-space identity; `within_bin=False` for the difference view — correct apart from P0-1, now fixed.
