
## Files

- `ARTICLE.md`: the article, rendered on tab 8.2; its numbers come from `scripts/paper_facts.py`.
- `LINKEDIN_POST.md`: the post, kept separate from the article.
- `ARTICLE_LONG_2026-09.md`: the long-form manuscript, historical source material. Not loaded by
  the app; tab 8.2 names it in a caption so a reader knows it exists.
- `figures/`: the article's figures and `facts.json`, regenerated by `scripts/` from the current
  code; `MANIFEST.md` lists them.
- `post/`: the post's images.
