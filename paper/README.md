
## Files

- `ARTICLE.md`: the article, rendered on tab 8.2; its numbers come from `scripts/paper_facts.py`
  and its figures are the app's own exports in `figures/`, used unchanged. Since 22 Sep 2026 this
  is the long form, about a fifth shorter than the manuscript it was cut down from; the 1 850-word
  article it replaced is `archive/superseded_notes/ARTICLE_SHORT_2026-09-22.md`.
- `LINKEDIN_POST.md`: the post, kept separate from the article. Written against the short article,
  so it is the next thing to rewrite.
- `ARTICLE_LONG_2026-09.md`: the 10 000-word manuscript `ARTICLE.md` was cut down from, kept as the
  source text. Not loaded by the app.
- `figures/`: every figure and table the app draws, exported by `scripts/export_exhibits.py` at
  the app's current defaults and named by number (`Figure_4.1.1a_…png`, `Table_5.1.4c_…png`);
  `MANIFEST.md` lists each with its caption and where in the app it comes from. `facts.json`
  and `prospect.json` are the run's numbers and the prospect behind them.
- The five bespoke paper figures and the app exports that preceded this set are in
  `archive/old_figures/superseded_2026-09-22/`.
